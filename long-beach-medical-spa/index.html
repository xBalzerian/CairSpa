Aerolase laser

One gentle laser, six common concerns

Aerolase is a medical laser that delivers energy in very short 650-microsecond pulses, which lets it treat a wide range of skin concerns with typically little discomfort and minimal downtime. At CAIR SPA in Huntington Beach, we use Aerolase for hair removal, skin tags, small veins, acne, melasma and pigmented spots, for adults of all skin types and tones.

How far is CAIR SPA from Long Beach?

CAIR SPA is about 20 miles from Long Beach, roughly 34 minutes by car without traffic. The usual route is I-405 south to the Brookhurst Street exit. We are at 20951 Brookhurst St, Ste 115, Huntington Beach.

Are you open on weekends?

Our regular hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM. Saturday and Sunday appointments are available by appointment, so call us to book a weekend visit.

Is the consultation free?

Yes. Every treatment starts with a free, no-obligation consultation, and you get an exact quote before anything begins.

Who oversees treatments at CAIR SPA?

Every service at CAIR SPA is performed under the oversight of Tran Plastic Surgery, led by Dr. Tuan Tran, a triple board-certified plastic, reconstructive and hand surgeon.
