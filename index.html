At a glance

CAIR SPA (Comprehensive Aesthetic Integrative Regeneration Spa) is a medical spa at 20951 Brookhurst St, Ste 115 in Huntington Beach, CA. It offers neurotoxin, HA fillers, Sculptra, PRF/PRP for hair and under-eyes, customized facials, microneedling and Aerolase laser treatments for hair removal, skin tags, veins, acne, melasma and pigmented lesions, plus IV and NAD+ therapy, medical weight loss and thread lifts. All services are performed under Tran Plastic Surgery. Consultations are free, Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM.

Aerolase laser

One gentle laser, six common concerns

Aerolase is a medical laser that delivers energy in very short 650-microsecond pulses, which lets it treat a wide range of skin concerns with typically little discomfort and minimal downtime. At CAIR SPA in Huntington Beach, we use Aerolase for hair removal, skin tags, small veins, acne, melasma and pigmented spots, for adults of all skin types and tones.

FAQ

Questions before your first visit

Can’t find your answer? Call us and we’ll help.

Call (949) 688-5898
Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Your first consultation at CAIR SPA is free and has no obligation. We listen to your goals, assess your skin and recommend a personalized plan with an exact quote before any treatment begins.

Who performs treatments at CAIR SPA?

All services at CAIR SPA are performed under Tran Plastic Surgery. Tran Plastic Surgery is led by Dr. Tuan Tran, a triple board-certified plastic, reconstructive and hand surgeon located in the same building.

How much do treatments cost?

Pricing depends on your goals, the treatment and the amount needed, so every plan is personalized. Book a free consultation and you will receive an exact quote before anything begins.

Where is CAIR SPA located?

CAIR SPA is at 20951 Brookhurst St, Suite 115, Huntington Beach, CA 92646, close to Fountain Valley and a short drive from Costa Mesa, Westminster and Newport Beach.

What are your hours?

We are open Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Call (949) 688-5898 or request a call back online to book.

Which treatments have little or no downtime?

Customized facials and IV therapy typically have no downtime. Neurotoxin and fillers may cause mild swelling or bruising for a day or two, and microneedling usually causes a few days of redness. Your provider will explain what to expect for your plan.
