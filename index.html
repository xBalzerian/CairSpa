At a glance

CAIR SPA (Comprehensive Aesthetic Integrative Regeneration Spa) is a medical spa at 20951 Brookhurst St, Suite 115 in Huntington Beach, CA. It offers neurotoxin, HA fillers, Sculptra, PRF/PRP for hair and under-eyes, microneedling, customized facials and Aerolase laser treatments for hair removal, skin tags, veins, acne, melasma and pigmented lesions, with free consultations Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM. All services are performed under Tran Plastic Surgery. Clients come from Huntington Beach, Fountain Valley, Costa Mesa, Newport Beach and across Orange County.

FAQ

Questions before your first visit

Still unsure? Call us. We’re happy to help Monday to Friday, 9 to 6.

Call (949) 688-5898
Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Your first consultation at CAIR SPA is free and comes with no obligation. We assess your goals, recommend a plan and give you a clear price before any treatment.

Where is CAIR SPA located?

We’re at 20951 Brookhurst St, Suite 115, Huntington Beach, CA 92646, near the Fountain Valley border, a short drive from Costa Mesa and Newport Beach.

What are your hours?

Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Call (949) 688-5898 or request a callback online to book.

Which treatment has the least downtime?

Customized facials have no downtime, so most clients go straight back to their day. Microneedling usually causes a few days of redness, and neurotoxin or fillers may cause mild swelling or bruising for a day or two. Your provider will explain what to expect for your treatment.

How much do treatments cost?

Pricing depends on your goals, the treatment and the amount needed, so every plan is personalized. Book a free consultation and you’ll get an exact quote before any treatment begins.
