Our Story

Born from a Passion for Transformation

CAIR SPA was founded on a simple belief: that advanced aesthetic medicine should be accessible, personalized, and transformative. Located in the heart of Huntington Beach, California, we serve clients from across Orange County who want real results without compromise.

CAIR stands for Comprehensive Aesthetic Integrative Regeneration — a philosophy that guides everything we do. We don't just treat symptoms; we address the whole person, integrating the latest in aesthetic science with a deeply personalized approach.

Our phoenix logo isn't just a symbol — it's our promise. Like a phoenix rising, we help our clients emerge renewed, radiant, and confident.
