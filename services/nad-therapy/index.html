NAD+ Therapy in Huntington Beach: Frequently Asked Questions

What is NAD+ and why do I need it?

NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) is a coenzyme found in every cell of your body. It is essential for energy production, DNA repair, and cellular health. NAD+ levels decline by up to 50% as we age, contributing to fatigue, cognitive decline, and aging-related issues.

How long does NAD+ therapy take?

NAD+ infusions typically take 2-4 hours depending on the dose and your tolerance. We start slowly to minimize any discomfort and gradually increase the rate as your body adjusts.

How often should I get NAD+ therapy?

For anti-aging and wellness, many clients start with a loading phase of 4-6 treatments over 2-4 weeks, then maintain with monthly sessions. For specific concerns like addiction recovery, more frequent sessions may be recommended initially.
