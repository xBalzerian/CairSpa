The Process

Three Steps to Instantly Glowing Skin

1

Cleanse + Peel

We begin with gentle exfoliation using a mix of glycolic and salicylic acids to uncover a fresh layer of skin. This removes dead skin cells and preps your skin for optimal serum absorption.

2

Extract + Hydrate

Using painless Vortex suction, we remove blackheads, whiteheads, and impurities from your pores while simultaneously infusing hydrating hyaluronic acid and antioxidants. No squeezing, no redness.

3

Fuse + Protect

We finish by saturating your skin with customized boosters and protective antioxidants. Choose from brightening, anti-aging, clarifying, or hydration boosters based on your skin goals.

FAQ

Signature Facial in Huntington Beach: Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a Signature Facial take?

A standard Signature Facial takes about 30-45 minutes, making it perfect for a lunch break treatment. Our Deluxe Signature Facial with additional boosters takes about 60 minutes.

How often should I get a Signature Facial?

For optimal results, we recommend monthly treatments to maintain your healthy, glowing skin. Many of our Huntington Beach clients book monthly Signature Facials as part of their skincare routine.

Can I wear makeup after my treatment?

Yes! One of the best things about Signature Facial is that you can apply makeup immediately after and return to your normal activities. Many clients come in during their lunch break.

How much does a Signature Facial cost in Huntington Beach?

The Signature Facial starts at $199. Add-on boosters for brightening, anti-aging, clarifying or deep hydration are priced at your visit, and we offer package pricing for monthly clients.

Is the Signature Facial safe for sensitive or acne-prone skin?

Yes. Because extraction uses gentle vortex suction instead of manual squeezing, the treatment is well tolerated by sensitive, rosacea-prone and acne-prone skin. Your aesthetician adjusts the peel strength and boosters to your skin type.
