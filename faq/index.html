Injectables & Fillers

How long does Botox last?

Botox typically lasts 3–4 months for most patients. Results vary based on the treatment area, dosage, and individual metabolism. Regular maintenance treatments help extend and optimize results.

How long do dermal fillers last?

Dermal fillers typically last 6–18 months depending on the type of filler and the treatment area. Lip fillers generally last 6–12 months, while cheek and jawline fillers can last 12–18 months or longer. Learn more on our Dermal Fillers Huntington Beach page.

Does Botox hurt?

Botox injections use a very fine needle and most patients describe only a mild pinching sensation. Treatment is quick — typically 10–15 minutes — with no downtime required.

Skin Treatments

How many HydraFacial sessions do I need?

Many clients see dramatic improvement after just one HydraFacial. For optimal and lasting results, we recommend a series of 3–6 treatments spaced 4 weeks apart, followed by monthly maintenance. Visit our HydraFacial Huntington Beach page for more details.

Is RF microneedling painful?

RF microneedling is well-tolerated by most patients. We apply a topical numbing cream 30–45 minutes before treatment. During the procedure, patients typically feel a warm, tingling sensation. See our RF Microneedling Huntington Beach page for more.

What is the downtime for RF microneedling?

Most patients experience mild redness and swelling for 24–48 hours post-treatment. Makeup can typically be applied the next day. Full results develop over 4–6 weeks as collagen remodeling occurs.

IV Therapy & Wellness

What is IV therapy used for?

IV therapy delivers vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and hydration directly into your bloodstream for 100% absorption. Common uses include energy optimization, immune support, hangover recovery, athletic recovery, anti-aging, and overall wellness. Explore our IV Therapy Huntington Beach page.

What is NAD+ therapy?

NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) is a coenzyme critical to cellular energy and repair. IV NAD+ therapy has been shown to support cognitive clarity, anti-aging, addiction recovery, and energy levels. Learn more on our NAD+ Therapy Huntington Beach page.

Weight Loss

What medical weight loss options do you offer?

We offer medically supervised weight loss programs including GLP-1 receptor agonist injections (such as semaglutide), personalized nutrition guidance, and IV therapy to support your metabolism. Visit our Medical Weight Loss Huntington Beach page.
